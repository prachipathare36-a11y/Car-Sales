# 🚗 Car Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Car Sales Analysis Dashboard is a Power BI project designed to analyze car sales data and understand sales performance across different companies, models, dealer regions, body styles, and customer segments.

The project uses a dataset containing **23,906 car sales records** with customer, vehicle, dealer, and pricing information.

The main objective of this project is to transform raw car sales data into meaningful insights through interactive data visualization.


## 📊 Dataset Information

The project uses two Excel files:

- **Car Sales.xlsx** – Main dataset containing complete car sales information.
- **Car.xlsx** – Additional dataset containing vehicle-related details

# 📖 Project Story

## About the Data

The dataset contains **23,906 car sales records** from January 2022 to December 2023.

It includes customer information, vehicle details, dealer information, car prices, body styles, engine types, transmission types, and dealer regions.

The data helps analyze car sales performance and understand factors that influence overall revenue and vehicle sales.


## 🔍 Business Problem Analysis

### 1. Which Car Companies Generate the Highest Sales?

The analysis compares total sales across different car companies to identify the highest revenue-generating brands.

### 2. Which Body Styles Generate the Highest Sales?

The analysis evaluates sales performance across different body styles to understand customer preferences.

### 3. How Does Sales Performance Differ Across Dealer Regions?

The analysis compares dealer regions to identify differences in sales performance and business opportunities.


## 📈 Key Data Insights

### 1. Total Sales

The dataset generated total sales of approximately **$671.53M** across **23,906 records**.

### 2. Leading Car Company

**Chevrolet** generated the highest total sales, contributing approximately **$47.66M**.

### 3. Most Valuable Body Style

**SUV** generated the highest total sales of approximately **$170.62M**, indicating strong customer demand for this body style.
